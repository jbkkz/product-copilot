# Request

The raw input — a real-shaped client email: a symptom, not a spec; three or four features
conflated into one; a hard constraint and a deadline buried at the end.

> Look, the whole event thing is chaos right now — we bring in freelancers to check guests in at
> the door, but nobody has a clear view of who's actually been approved to attend versus who just
> shows up, so people slip through and others get turned away by mistake. Then afterwards finance
> spends weeks reconciling because the freelancer invoices never line up with the hours actually
> worked, and half their contracts weren't even signed before the event. We need something that
> ties this together — the door staff should see approval status live, someone should get alerted
> when there's a mismatch, and at the end it should all reconcile cleanly so finance stops chasing
> us. It has to work at the venue where the wifi basically doesn't. Event's in six weeks.
