- **Requivo is 1.0.** The number is a statement about the interface, not a claim that the product is
  finished: from 1.0.0 onward a breaking change to the public surface costs a major version and is
  announced before it lands. `docs/compatibility.md` now says which four things that surface is — the
  session format, the `--json` outputs, the error-code vocabulary, and the CLI verbs, flags and exit
  codes — and what a 2.0 would have to contain (#79).
- The promise is deliberately bounded, and the boundary is the part worth reading. Python internals,
  prompt and context-card content, and terminal output layout are explicitly *not* covered and are
  tuned continuously; a consumer depending on them is tracking the repo on purpose. A promise that
  absorbs everything is one nobody can keep (#79).
- Nothing on that page gets looser at 1.0. "Nothing is removed in a patch" and "anything deprecated
  keeps working for at least one minor version and names its replacement" were held throughout 0.x
  and are stronger than SemVer asks of a 1.0 — the number was the part that lagged, not the practice
  (#79).
