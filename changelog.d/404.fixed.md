  way a provider does it -- not a guessed number. The published "One provider call" row widens from
  7,300-8,900 to 8,000-11,000 input tokens; every dollar figure downstream of it in `docs/providers.md`
  moves with it, arithmetically, and the test that recomputes both is what keeps them agreeing. The
  README's own `$0.03 to $0.06 per call` and `under $1` claims are unchanged by the correction -- they
  already had enough rounding margin to still hold.
