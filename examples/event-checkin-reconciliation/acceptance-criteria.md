# Event Check-in & Freelancer Reconciliation — Acceptance Criteria

> Acceptance criteria — generated by Requivo

## Live guest check-in at the door

### [ ] AC-1 — Door staff see live approval status on scan  _Happy path_

- **Given** A guest is on the pre-event approval list with status 'approved'
- **And** Door staff have the check-in app open on their device
- **When** Staff scan the guest's badge/QR code (or search their name)
- **Then** The guest's approval status is displayed immediately on screen
- **And** A check-in event is recorded for that guest, timestamped

### [ ] AC-2 — Check-in continues to work when venue wifi is unavailable  _Edge case_

- **Given** The device holds a local snapshot of the approval list downloaded before the event
- **And** The venue has no usable network connection at the time of scanning
- **When** Staff scan a guest while the device is offline
- **Then** The guest's approval status is resolved from the local snapshot without requiring a live connection
- **And** The check-in is recorded locally and marked as pending sync
- **And** Once connectivity returns, the pending check-in syncs to the central record without data loss or duplication

### [ ] AC-3 — Guest not on the approval list is clearly flagged  _Error_

- **Given** A person presents themselves at the door with no matching entry on the approval list
- **When** Staff attempt to check them in by scan or name search
- **Then** The app clearly shows a 'not approved' / 'not found' state, visually distinct from an approved state
- **And** Entry is not silently treated as approved based on a blank or ambiguous result

### [ ] AC-4 — Last-minute walk-in can be handled on the spot  _Edge case_

- **Given** A person arrives without a prior registration or approval record
- **When** Staff attempt to check them in
- **Then** Staff have a way to register or flag the walk-in on the spot rather than only rejecting them
- **And** The resulting record is distinguishable afterward from a pre-approved guest

## Access & visibility boundaries

### [ ] AC-5 — Door staff have read-only access to approval data  _Permission_

- **Given** A door staff member is logged into the check-in app during the event
- **When** They view a guest's record while checking someone in
- **Then** They can see the guest's approval status
- **And** They cannot edit the underlying approval list, contract terms, or invoice data from this screen

### [ ] AC-6 — A mismatch alert reaches a responsible person  _Edge case_

- **Given** A discrepancy is detected during or after the event (e.g. an unexpected check-in pattern or a reconciliation gap)
- **When** The mismatch condition is triggered
- **Then** A notification is expected to reach a designated responsible person rather than being logged silently
- **And** The exact rule that defines a mismatch and the recipient's role should be confirmed before this is treated as settled behaviour

## Freelancer hours, contract & invoice reconciliation

### [ ] AC-7 — Post-event reconciliation report links check-in, hours and invoice  _Happy path_

- **Given** Freelancer shifts, contract terms and invoice data exist for the event
- **When** Finance runs the post-event reconciliation at close
- **Then** A report is expected to show, per freelancer, the link between hours/mission recorded and the corresponding invoice line
- **And** Discrepancies between recorded activity and invoiced amount are expected to be visible on this report rather than requiring manual cross-checking
- **And** This scenario should be confirmed with the client as the concrete definition of 'reconciles cleanly' before being signed off

### [ ] AC-8 — Every check-in and reconciliation change is traceable  _Edge case_

- **Given** Check-ins have occurred and reconciliation adjustments have been made after the event
- **When** Someone reviews the record for a given guest or freelancer
- **Then** It is expected to be possible to see who did what and when for each check-in and each reconciliation entry
- **And** This audit trail should be confirmed as a hard requirement given the compliance sensitivity flagged for this build

## Concurrency & duplicate handling

### [ ] AC-9 — Same guest scanned twice within seconds at different doors  _Edge case_

- **Given** Two staff members at different entrances scan the same guest within a few seconds of each other, potentially both offline
- **When** Both scans are eventually synced to the central record
- **Then** The system is expected to produce one defined, non-ambiguous outcome (e.g. a flagged duplicate) rather than two silently accepted independent check-ins
- **And** The precise rule for duplicate vs. re-entry vs. different-zone access should be confirmed before this is treated as a fixed criterion

## Open questions

- What exactly defines a 'mismatch' — guest check-ins against the approval list, freelancer hours against contract/invoice terms, or both — and does each have its own owner and notification channel, as the client has not separated these?
- Who specifically receives a mismatch alert (event manager, finance, both), and through which channel?
- Can a freelancer whose contract is not yet signed still work the door on event day, or must this be blocked — the client has not ruled on this compliance gap?
- Is freelancer time genuinely meant to be clocked in/out and reconciled against invoiced hours, or should the mismatch check be against mission/deliverable terms instead — clocking hours for a freelancer carries a disguised-employment (salariat déguisé) risk under French labor law that should be checked with legal/HR before building it.
- Does the check-in app need to show a visible 'pending/unconfirmed' state during offline operation, or must every scan resolve to a certain result before the guest is let through?
- Do freelancer contracts, rates and invoicing already live in an existing HR/contract system that this app must integrate with, or will they be entered fresh for this event?
- Is this build a one-off for this single event, or the first version of a reusable kit for future events — this changes the data model and how much configurability is worth investing in given the six-week deadline?
- What is the exact acceptance scenario that proves finance's 'reconciles cleanly' requirement is met — a specific report format, a matching threshold, or a sign-off step?
- Given the fixed six-week deadline and dual scope (door check-in and freelancer reconciliation), should freelancer reconciliation ship as a post-event report only, with live mismatch alerting descoped unless proven necessary?
